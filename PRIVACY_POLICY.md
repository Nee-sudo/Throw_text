# Privacy Policy — Throw-Your-Text

**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

Throw-Your-Text ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a simple web application that allows users to share text between devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use Throw-Your-Text.

## 1. Information We Collect

Throw-Your-Text is designed to minimize the amount of personal information required to use the service.

### Text Content

When you submit text through Throw-Your-Text, the text may be temporarily stored on our server/database so that it can be accessed from another device.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, or other confidential information through the service.

### Automatically Collected Information

When you access the website, our hosting/server infrastructure or third-party services used by the application may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device type
- Operating system
- Date and time of requests
- Basic usage or error information

This information may be used for security, debugging, performance monitoring, and preventing abuse.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected through Throw-Your-Text may be used to:

- Provide and operate the text-sharing service
- Store and retrieve submitted text
- Maintain and improve the application
- Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized activity
- Troubleshoot technical problems
- Maintain the security and reliability of the service

We do not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to use the basic text-sharing functionality.

## 3. Data Retention

Text submitted to Throw-Your-Text may be stored temporarily to provide the sharing functionality.

We may delete stored text according to the application's data-retention or cleanup mechanisms. However, we do not guarantee that all submitted content will be deleted within a specific period unless a specific expiration period is stated by the application.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

Throw-Your-Text may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to remember preferences or support application functionality.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as:

- Remembering user preferences
- Maintaining application settings
- Improving the user experience

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some functionality may not work correctly as a result.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Throw-Your-Text may use third-party infrastructure or services for hosting, databases, analytics, security, or other technical functionality.

These providers may process limited technical information as necessary to provide their services.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the service. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through Throw-Your-Text.

## 7. User Responsibility

Throw-Your-Text is a text-sharing utility. Users are responsible for the content they submit.

You should not use the service to share:

- Passwords or authentication credentials
- Banking or payment information
- Highly sensitive personal information
- Illegal or harmful content
- Content that violates another person's privacy or rights

## 8. Children's Privacy

Throw-Your-Text is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 9. Your Choices

Depending on the functionality available in the application, you may be able to delete or stop sharing content that you have submitted.

You may also stop using the service at any time.

For requests concerning stored information, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, technology, legal requirements, or our practices.

When changes are made, the **"Last Updated"** date at the top of this policy will be updated.
