About the ocean

Every bottle carries a thought

This is a place where people can release ideas, experiences, questions and thoughts into a shared digital ocean — and, in turn, stumble on something a stranger has set adrift.

It began with a simple question: what if the internet could hold something quieter than a feed? A space where the unspoken could be spoken, without a name attached to it, without an audience watching. So the ocean was built — a place to set adrift a belief, an unconventional idea, or a raw experience, and to read what others have released in return.
